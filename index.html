Paolo Alberto Baudino

PhD candidate in Economics at Bocconi University, focused on macroeconomics and finance. Previously a research analyst at the European Central Bank. My research interests lie at the intersection of macroeconomics and finance, particularly how the evolving financial landscape shapes monetary policy transmission, credit supply, and financial stability, as well as the implications of monetary policy for the green transition.
